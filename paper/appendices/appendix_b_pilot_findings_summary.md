## Interpretation

The pilot does not currently support a simple claim that public providers are broadly wrong. Instead, the early evidence supports a more balanced and defensible claim: provider EV can be close to annual-report reconstructed EV in cleaner cases, but moderate divergence appears when cash-like investments, lease liabilities, consolidation adjustments, or working-capital financing instruments are material.

## Company-Level Notes

### Bharat Electronics Ltd

BEL currently acts as a reliability benchmark. Preliminary StockAnalysis EV is close to reconstructed EV, with a deviation around 0.5%. This suggests that for large, clean, widely covered companies, provider EV can be reasonably close to a conservative reconstructed EV bridge.

### NALCO

NALCO acts as a cash-rich commodity case. Visible provider EV observations show moderate deviation, generally in the 2% to 5% bucket. The main question is whether providers subtract a broader set of cash-like assets, apply a different balance-sheet date, or treat leases differently.

### Polycab India Ltd

Polycab acts as a premium compounder and accounting-treatment sensitivity case. The conservative bridge is close to StockAnalysis EV, but the treatment of acceptances changes the interpretation. Because acceptances are linked to usance letters of credit and working-capital financing, the paper should disclose them separately and present a sensitivity rather than force them into the base EV bridge.

### Hero MotoCorp Ltd

Hero MotoCorp acts as a mature large-cap case with substantial current investments and consolidation adjustments. The preliminary StockAnalysis comparison falls in the moderate-deviation bucket. The key research question is whether provider EV consistently subtracts current investments and whether lease liabilities and non-controlling interests are included.

## Go / No-Go Assessment

The pilot has enough signal to continue, but the framing should remain cautious.

Current decision:

```text
Proceed cautiously toward an expanded sample, but frame the paper as a reliability-and-exceptions study rather than a provider-error study.
```
